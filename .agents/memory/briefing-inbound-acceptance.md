# Briefing podcast — inbound mail acceptance policy

The webhook (`handleInboundMessage` in `routes/briefingPodcast.ts`) decides what
becomes a podcast. The order and intent:

1. **Approved-sender allow-list is the trust boundary.** A row in
   `briefingPodcastClientsTable` with `status="approved"` is human-curated trust.
   Everything downstream trusts that the sender intends to send briefings.
2. **Accept auto-generated / redirected briefings.** Real briefings are
   machine-generated newsletters that users auto-forward or Exchange-redirect
   into the mailbox. Redirects preserve the ORIGINAL briefing's
   `Auto-Submitted: auto-generated` and `X-Auto-Response-Suppress` headers and
   produce NO `Fwd:`/`Re:` subject prefix. So we must NOT reject on those.
   **Why:** the old guard rejected `isAutoGeneratedEmail()` (any of In-Reply-To /
   Auto-Submitted≠no / X-Auto-Response-Suppress / X-MS-Exchange-Generated-
   Message-By) unless subject started with `Fw:` — which silently dropped every
   redirected briefing. User explicitly chose "make it automatic".
3. **Still block genuine machine mail that would loop or is meaningless:**
   `isAutoReplyOrSystemMessage()` (in `lib/storage/spe/graphMail.ts`) blocks only
   `Auto-Submitted: auto-replied|auto-notified` (OOF/vacation) and
   `X-MS-Exchange-Generated-Message-By` (NDRs, read receipts, delivery reports).
4. **Block our own delivery email (loop guard).** The pipeline emails the
   recipient back with subject `Your briefing podcast — …`. A recipient's broad
   forward/redirect rule can bounce that back into the mailbox from an approved
   sender → re-ingested → re-podcasted → infinite loop. Guard rejects subjects
   starting with `BRIEFING_PODCAST_EMAIL_SUBJECT_PREFIX` (exported from
   `lib/email.ts`; used for both the inbound guard and the outbound subject so
   they can't drift).

**How to apply:** any change to what the briefing webhook accepts must preserve
both directions — let redirected/auto-generated briefings through, but never let
OOF/system mail or our own `Your briefing podcast — …` delivery mail re-enter the
pipeline. The outbound subject prefix and the inbound guard MUST stay tied to the
same shared constant.
