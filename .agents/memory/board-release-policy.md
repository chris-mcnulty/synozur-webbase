---
name: Board release policy
description: Board preannouncement privacy and content ownership requirements.
---

The Board of Advisors must stay restricted to signed-in administrators until an administrator explicitly publishes it. Adding content, running seeds/migrations, merging or redeploying is not release approval.

**Why:** The user requires a private preannouncement section and has not provided external advisor names, biographies or portraits. Do not invent them.

**How to apply:** Future Board imports and editorial work must preserve visibility. Sharing profile information does not imply Team membership or permission to publish the Board.

Team and Board membership must be independent, explicit CMS controls. Do not automatically enroll Michelle, Chris, or anyone else by name.

**Why:** The user rejected hard-coded enrollment and approved independent membership controls. A former Team member must be able to appear on Board without reactivating Team membership.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing profiles and memberships. Linked entries share identity, photo and biography; changing one membership must not change the other or publish the Board.
