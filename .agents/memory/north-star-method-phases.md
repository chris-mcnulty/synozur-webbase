name: North Star Method phase names
description: The canonical four phase names of the North Star Method, and a known doc collision.
---

The North Star Method™ four phases are **Assess · Define · Deliver · Outcomes**.

**Why:** This is what the live Synozur site (`home.tsx` method section) and the Galaxy
customer portal (`artifacts/galaxy/src/components/lifecycle.tsx`) both use, and the user
confirmed it is correct.

**Collision to watch:** The May 2026 Messaging & Positioning Framework docx proposes
renaming the phases to "Orient · Align · Activate · Adopt". That is an aspirational
proposal, NOT the shipped product. Do not change phase names to Orient/Align/Activate/Adopt
unless the user explicitly approves the rename. `replit.md` previously had the wrong
(Orient/Align/Activate/Adopt) names; corrected to Assess/Define/Deliver/Outcomes.
