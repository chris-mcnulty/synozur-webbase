A conversation grounded in your Nation’s priorities

For U.S. Tribal Nations and Canadian First Nations, practical AI adoption begins with context, relationships, and trust. This initial conversation is a listening session to understand your goals, constraints, and where AI could support community priorities.

What we can discuss

  • Your priorities and the people or programs the work should support.
  • Current AI use, foundational skills, and opportunities to build local capability.
  • Trust, data boundaries, human oversight, and Nation-defined governance.
  • Whether a Champion Readout, the Aurora champion-network program, or a Champion Module within the AI & North Star Sprint could be an appropriate next step.

What this appointment includes

We will listen to your priorities, discuss your context, and consider next steps together. An Orion assessment, Champion Readout, or delivery engagement is scoped separately and is not included simply by booking this conversation.

Before we meet

Bring a priority you would like to explore and consider which leadership, program, or digital colleagues should join. You do not need to have selected an AI platform.

Please keep booking notes high-level. Do not include sensitive community information, personal records, or restricted cultural material.

Your appointment may be with any available Synozur adviser. Michelle Boyd coordinates follow-up for tribal-business inquiries.
