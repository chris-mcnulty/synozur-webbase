SPRINT PAGE OPENING (READY TO USE)
🔷 HEADLINE (Reframe)
The problem isn’t how fast you’re moving.
It’s that your leadership team isn’t aligned on what matters most.
________________________________________
🔷 SUBHEAD (Analytical, structured)
As organizations scale, complexity increases faster than clarity.
Decisions fragment, priorities compete, and execution slows—not because teams lack capability, but because alignment has eroded.
What looks like an execution problem is almost always a clarity problem.
________________________________________
🔷 DIAGNOSTIC TENSION (Precision, not narrative)
You are likely seeing some of this already:
•	Leadership discussions that don’t resolve into clear decisions
•	Strategic priorities that shift faster than teams can execute
•	AI initiatives that exist—but don’t meaningfully change outcomes
•	Strong teams working hard, but not in the same direction
•	Growth introducing friction instead of momentum
These are not isolated issues.
They are signals of a missing or misaligned North Star.
________________________________________
🔷 REFRAME LOCK-IN (This is the “advisor moment”)
Without a clearly defined and consistently applied North Star:
•	Execution becomes reactive
•	AI amplifies noise instead of clarity
•	Leadership alignment degrades under scale
The issue isn’t effort.
It’s that the system guiding that effort isn’t explicit enough to scale.
________________________________________
🔷 TRANSITION TO THE SPRINT
This is where the Sprint begins.
