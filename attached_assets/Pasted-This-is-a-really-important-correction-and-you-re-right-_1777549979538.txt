✅ REVISED PROMPT FOR THE REPLIT AGENT
(Essence‑driven, not numerology‑driven)
You are constructing the Synozur home page.

Context:
- Synozur is positioned as “The Transformation Company.”
- Internally, Synozur operates with a disciplined strategy across:
  - Core transformation pillars
  - Proprietary software platforms
  - High‑impact workshops
  - Clearly defined ideal customer profiles
- This structure should be FELT in the experience, not explained or enumerated.

Goal:
Ensure the home page communicates strategic coherence, depth, and confidence
— without exposing internal frameworks or forcing numeric symmetry.

Guiding principle:
Do NOT explain the 4×4×4×4 strategy.
DO express its underlying logic:
clarity → assessment → action → outcomes.

Primary implementation guidance:

1. Pillars should be implied through language, not listed as “four”
   - Use natural language that signals:
     - Strategic clarity
     - Human‑centered change
     - Technology as an enabler
     - Measurable outcomes
   - Avoid explicit counts, diagrams, or “our four pillars” copy.
   - The visitor should intuit the structure without being taught it.

2. Software should appear as enabling systems, not products
   - Vega, Orion, and Orbit should be presented as:
     - Ways Synozur operationalizes thinking
     - Tools that guide leaders, not things to buy
   - Do not introduce them as a “suite” or list them numerically.
   - Orion should quietly feel like a natural starting point.

3. Workshops should signal momentum, not transactions
   - Workshops should feel like:
     - Moments of alignment
     - Accelerators of clarity
     - Safe entry points into transformation
   - Avoid framing workshops as SKUs or offers.
   - Use language that emphasizes outcomes and decisions, not duration or format.

4. Ideal customers should recognize themselves without being named
   - Write for:
     - Executives leading complex change
     - Organizations navigating AI, scale, or market shifts
   - Do not enumerate industries or personas unless already present.
   - The copy should say “this is for leaders like me” without stating it.

5. The carousel should function as evidence of coherence
   - The carousel already contains:
     - Models (how Synozur thinks)
     - Applications (how Synozur enables)
     - Case studies (how Synozur proves)
     - Content (how Synozur teaches)
   - Add only light framing so visitors understand:
     “This is how Synozur works in the real world.”
   - Do not describe the carousel as a framework, library, or feed.

6. Maintain MPF tone at all times
   - Calm, confident, advisory
   - Human‑centered and empathetic
   - No hype, no urgency language, no over‑structuring
   - Avoid explicit marketing constructs (funnels, stages, counts)

Success criteria:
- A first‑time executive visitor should feel:
  “This firm understands complexity and has a system for navigating it.”
- A returning visitor should quickly find proof, insight, or a way to engage.
- The experience should feel intentional, not explained.
